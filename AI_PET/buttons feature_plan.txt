AI BUDDY - APPS MENU NOTES (2026-10-18)

BOARD
- Generic ESP32 DevKit V1 (WROOM-32). PSRAM test printed 0 bytes, so GPIO 16/17 are free.
- Only one side of the board is reachable on the breadboard:
  D15, D2, D4, RX2, TX2, D5, D18, D19, D21, RX0, TX0, D22, D23
- D21/D22 = I2C (OLED + MPU6050).

BUTTONS (6 total, wired to GND, INPUT_PULLUP, no resistors)
- UP     = GPIO 19
- DOWN   = GPIO 18
- LEFT   = GPIO 17 (TX2)
- RIGHT  = GPIO 16 (RX2)
- CENTER = GPIO 4  (really a SELECT/A button)
- EXIT   = GPIO 23
- TX2/RX2 are plain GPIOs as long as Serial2 is never started.

PHYSICAL LAYOUT
- UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT in a "+" shape with an empty middle.
- CENTER and EXIT are a separate pair placed together.
- Risk: EXIT next to CENTER can be hit by accident during a game.
  Keep a gap of at least one button width, put EXIT on the outer side,
  and make it feel different (colour or height).

PINS REJECTED
- GPIO 1, 3 (TX0/RX0): USB serial and debug log
- GPIO 15: boot strapping pin
- GPIO 2: strapping pin and onboard LED
- GPIO 5: strapping pin (kept as a spare)
- Spares for later: D5, D2

NAVIGATION FLOW
- Eyes --CENTER--> Apps menu
- Apps menu: UP/DOWN moves the highlight
- Apps menu --CENTER--> App running
- App running --EXIT button--> Apps menu
- Apps menu --EXIT button or EXIT row--> Eyes
- The framework reads EXIT before any app, so apps never handle exit.
- Apps own the 4 arrow buttons and CENTER (LEFT is used in Timer and future games).
- EXIT triggers on release; presses shorter than ~30 ms are ignored (bounce only).

DECISIONS
- Rejected: hold CENTER to exit (the game reacts before the hold ends).
- Rejected: LDR exit (tuning, false triggers).
- Chosen: a dedicated physical EXIT button.
- Rejected: LVGL for icons (overkill for 128x64, does not use Adafruit GFX).
- Icons: 1-bit PROGMEM bitmaps drawn with drawBitmap().
- Confirmed: CENTER opens the menu from the eyes.
- Confirmed: the EXIT button also works from the menu and goes to the eyes
  (same result as the menu's EXIT row, which stays).
